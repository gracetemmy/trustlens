What just happened

Reading the output.

🎯

The score

Probability that this position breaches its liquidation threshold within seven days. One narrow, falsifiable question — not a vague "risk rating" that can never be wrong.

📊

The attribution

Each row is a real feature contribution to the model's log-odds, sorted by magnitude and regenerated on every change. It isn't written copy — move a slider and watch the ordering rearrange itself.

🔒

The payload

What the chain actually sees of your private evidence: an opaque blob. The score and LTV cap come back signed; the inputs never leave the enclave.
